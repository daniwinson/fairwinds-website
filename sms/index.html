Charter Client Text Messages

Fairwinds, LLC (Fairwinds Yacht Charters, Newport Beach, CA) texts charter clients from (949) 997-2749 about their charter inquiries and bookings: replies to your questions, booking confirmations, reminders, and weather or schedule updates. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

How to opt in

  • Check the optional text-message consent box next to the Phone field on our charter inquiry form, or
  • Tell us by phone or email that you'd like to receive texts, or
  • Text us first at (949) 997-2749.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of booking.

Message frequency & rates

Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per booking. Msg & data rates may apply.

How to opt out or get help

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. You'll receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or contact dale@fairwindsnewport.com or (949) 997-2749. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.

Vessel owners and crew: the separate yacht-maintenance text line is described at yacht.fairwindsnewport.com/sms.
